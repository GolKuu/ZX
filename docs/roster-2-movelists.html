Circle Clash Ultimate · roster sheet 02

Velocity King & Elastic Brawler

Naming & design Both movesets map onto roster slots that already existed in CHR-CCU-300 — frame manipulation was always Kade Ruven's power, elastic impact absorption was always Tamsin Oduya's. Every technique is renamed; mechanics carry over frame-for-frame. Two design substitutions: the brimmed hat is a rigger's wide-brim rather than a straw hat, since that specific item is a registered mark and not a costume detail; and the Level 3 transformation is a smeared many-limbed form rather than a worm — identical full-screen horizontal crush hitboxes, different silhouette.

Cross-character

Animation standards

  • Everything is on 2s and 3s. Neutral poses hold. Impact frames hold. Interpolation is disabled on any frame flagged as a key — the export pipeline must not optimise repeated keys away or the entire style collapses into smooth 3D.
  • Smear frames are geometry, not motion blur. Each character carries 4–7 swap meshes bound to the same skeleton, shown for 1–3 frames. Velocity King gets the maximum seven; his whole identity is the frames he leaves behind.
  • Elastic deformation is crash-test, never rubber-hose. Tamsin's limbs taper and snap back with a 2–3 frame overshoot past neutral. Reference is a dummy taking a barrier hit, not a bouncing ball. The animation never mugs for a laugh.
  • Every Level 3 ends on the cost. Beat one is the decision, beat two is the technique, beat three is the character visibly paying for it. Beat three is protected from cuts.
  • Command throws get long animations on purpose. Presentation runs 96 frames and most of them are him being unhurried. The humiliation is the mechanic and it needs the screen time.
  • Root motion is baked to displacement curves at build time. The simulation moves the character; animation never does.
