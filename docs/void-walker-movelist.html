Void Walker

Sesa Nour · 19 · The Fold
840Health

Born in the Fold, never included in the agreement that binds everyone else. Space has no arrangement with him — he arrives without crossing the distance, and he cannot always stop. Dominant in neutral, catastrophic once his gauge empties, and structurally unable to Clash.

Naming Every technique is renamed. Mechanics carry over unchanged — attraction, repulsion, teleportation and nullification are not protectable; a specific character and their named techniques are. Silhouette keeps the brief: tall, lean, white hair, high-collar coat, violet facet lighting. The eye covering is an angled visor rather than a horizontal wrap, since that one element combined with white hair is the part that reads as somebody else's character.

Unique mechanic

Exemption

Hold ↓ ↓ + Block and he lowers his hands. A barely visible distortion settles around him and incoming attacks stop ten centimetres short — the opponent reads MISSED, takes no blockstun credit, and completes their full recovery.

Gauge

100

Regenerates 4 per second in neutral. 60-frame lockout after being hit.

Cost — projectile

20

Five projectiles empties him from full.

Cost — melee

35

Two melee hits empties him from full.

Minimum to function

20

Below this it is offline entirely.

Exemption is OFF for every frame of every attack he throws
  • Pressing a button turns his defence off. Every poke, every combo, every conversion is spent mortal. Whiff-punishing him is not a read — it is the correct answer, always available.
  • Throws pierce it. So do PIERCE-flagged moves at 60% reduced efficiency, unblockables, and all Level 3 supers.
  • Absorption is not a block. He takes no blockstun and is left at neutral while the attacker finishes recovering — absorbing a −5 move hands him a free five-frame punish.
  • He cannot Clash. Having nothing to agree with, his attacks carry no priority class. Where anyone else would Clash, he trades and both fighters take damage. This costs him the Clash Cancel, the meter reward, and the escape.

Reading the routes Vacuum deals zero damage on purpose — every long route runs through it, and every one of those frames is spent with Exemption offline. His damage ceiling and his defensive uptime are the same resource, which is the entire character.
For the opponent

How to beat him

  • Throw him. Throws pierce Exemption, he has no invincible reversal, and he has 840 health. A grappler who walks forward and blocks wins this matchup.
  • Never throw single projectiles. You are feeding him meter for nothing. Zone with PIERCE moves or do not zone at all.
  • Whiff-punish the buttons, not the neutral. His attack recovery is the only place he loses, and it is available on every single move.
  • Drain, then commit. Two melee absorptions empty him. The 60-frame regen lockout means continuous pressure holds him at zero for the rest of the round.
  • Respect the absorb. If your hit gets eaten you are minus and he is neutral. Stop the string. Do not double down.
  • Bait Repel · Field. Thirty-four frames of startup and −20 on block. Block it once and take a full combo.
